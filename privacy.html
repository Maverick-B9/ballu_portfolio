Who I am

This website is a personal portfolio run by Balaram B., Mysore, Karnataka, India ("I", "me"). I am the person responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach me at ballubalaram2003@gmail.com.

I do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, social-media widgets or user accounts. I do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data. Please do not put any in the contact form.

How I use it

Contact-form details are used only to read and answer your message, and to keep a record of the conversation. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box before sending. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing me. I do not sell your data, use it for marketing, or add you to mailing lists.

Who receives it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). The contact form is delivered through this email-forwarding service, which passes your message to my inbox. Its own privacy policy applies to its handling.
  • Email provider. Messages arrive in a Gmail inbox operated by Google.
  • Hosting provider. The site is served by a static hosting provider, which keeps routine server logs.
  • Google Fonts. The pages load the Manrope and DM Mono typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore sends your IP address and browser details to Google when a page loads.

Some of these providers are located outside India, so your data may be processed in other countries. I do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long I keep it

Emails from the contact form are kept for as long as needed to deal with your message and any follow-up, and normally deleted within 12 months of the last contact unless we are working together. Hosting logs follow the host's own retention schedule.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, for visitors in the EU/UK, the GDPR, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. Email me and I will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within 30 days.

Grievance contact: Balaram B., Mysore, Karnataka, India · ballubalaram2003@gmail.com

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent me information, email me and I will delete it.

Changes

If I change this policy I will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be described here.

Contact
