ACCESSIBILITY.

This site should be usable by everyone. Here is what I aim for, what is in place and what is still imperfect.

LAST UPDATED 26 OCTOBER 2026

My aimWhat is in placeMotionKnown limitationsFeedback

My aim

I aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. I test it by hand and with automated checks, but I have not had it audited by a third party, so I cannot claim full conformance.

What is in place

  • Semantic headings, landmarks and a "skip to content" link.
  • Full keyboard operation of the menu, links and form, with visible focus.
  • Labelled form fields, inline error messages and a polite live status for results.
  • Touch targets of at least 44 px for buttons and menu controls on phones.
  • Responsive layouts from 320 px wide, and text that can be zoomed.
  • Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers; diagrams have the same information in text.

Motion

If your device is set to "reduce motion", the animations are switched off or replaced by static versions, including the scroll effects, particles and simulations.

Known limitations

  • The interactive simulations are drawn on a canvas. They are decorative, labelled as such, and the case-study text carries the same information.
  • Some small monospaced labels use low-emphasis grey on dark. I am working on raising their contrast.
  • The PDF resume may not be fully tagged for screen readers. Ask me for another format.

Feedback

If something is hard to use, tell me at ballubalaram2003@gmail.com and I will try to fix it or offer the content another way.
