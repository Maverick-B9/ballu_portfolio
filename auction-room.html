01THE PROBLEM

A live auction needs a clear view of bids, player roles, team composition and remaining purse.

Many people bid at once, and every bid changes what a team can afford next. A lost or duplicated bid is not a small bug: it changes who wins a player. The system has to stay correct while it is busy.

02THE APPROACH

A production-ready platform built with Next.js, React, TypeScript and MySQL, with live bidding and automated purse management.

It was deployed at Mahadasara 2025 and served 2,500+ participants across multiple teams, with zero data loss throughout concurrent, high-frequency bidding.

WORKS

A simplified view of the flow, step by step.

  1. Bid

    A team places a bid in the live auction room.

  2. Check

    The bid is checked against the team's remaining purse.

  3. Record

    The accepted bid is stored in MySQL.

  4. Show

    Everyone in the room sees the updated state.
