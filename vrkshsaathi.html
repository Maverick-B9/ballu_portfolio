01THE PROBLEM

Planting is only the beginning.

Young trees need follow-up, accountability and local participation to survive. Planting drives are easy to photograph; the months afterwards are not. VrkshSaathi explores how software could keep that second part from being forgotten.

02THE APPROACH

Proposed stewardship workflows: geotagged planting records, species knowledge, citizen reporting and hierarchical follow-up.

The design principle is to make responsibility visible, local and verifiable.

03HOW IT
WORKS

A simplified view of the flow, step by step.

  1. Plant

    A geotagged record is created for each tree.

  2. Know

    Species knowledge tells people how to care for it.

  3. Report

    Citizens report on a tree's condition.

  4. Follow up

    Reports reach the right level of follow-up.

NOTEAll of this is proposed. It is not a deployed system.
